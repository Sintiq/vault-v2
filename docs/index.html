Before you install

This first release has no paid Windows publisher certificate, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher or SmartScreen warning. Do not disable system protection. If you do not trust the download, do not run it. Keep backups of important files.

Useful on your PC. Optional on your phone.

Bring your archive together

Import files, arrange folders and shelves, preview supported documents, and restore items from Trash. Start without AI or a phone.

Get help finding things

Optional local AI suggests categories and helps with document questions. You review its suggestions. Ollama and the model are installed separately.

Take only what you choose

View files on a paired Android phone. With the PC online, take files from Staging for offline use, Save or Share. You choose each action; the agent does not send documents for you.

Know what stays local — and what does not.

  • Local document AI uses Ollama. The separate, explicitly selected Claude Code chat option is a cloud service, not local-only mode.
  • Phone access uses your Tailscale network. Paired-phone access, configured reminders and chosen exports can transfer data; local-first is not a promise that nothing leaves the PC.
  • You can revoke paired devices on the PC. Copies already saved or shared outside Vault cannot be recalled.
  • The phone PIN is not full-disk encryption of your PC. Nine wrong PIN attempts erase Vault's app-local data on that phone, not the PC originals.
  • Updates are voluntary and checked against signed release manifests. They do not install automatically. Health records are not medical advice.
